# 0.5.46 — 2026-11-16

Everything merged since 0.5.45, by area:

- **Health:** a daily health report at 07:00: green, amber or red with the reason, on Health → Daily report, in Home Assistant, and on phones when amber or red.
- **Analytics:** Explain reads clearly, links to Billing & Utilities and gives the grid effect the right way round; the History card shows the chart's period; the SoC axis shows only with SoC; Lifetime is bars only.
- **Billing & Utilities:** Import tariff says what it added and has Done; no second import.
- **Changelog and README:** the release index has its own heading in HA's update dialog; the Add repository button is at the top of the README.

### Added
- **Daily health report** (phase 1 of the scheduled health check, as in the Modbus Bridge). At 07:00 site time the bridge checks its workers, polls, alerts, log and housekeeping over the last 24 hours against the 7 days before, and reports green, amber or red with the reason first. It is on **Health → Daily report** (with Run now and earlier reports), in one Home Assistant notification that the next replaces, and on the phones only when amber or red (new trigger: **Daily health report amber or red**). A daily snapshot of each gateway's totals is kept for good for the trends to come. Switch: **07:00 each day**. See [Daily health report](../guide/health-report.md).

### Changed
- **README:** the **Add repository to Home Assistant** button is at the top of the README, not only in [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/david2069/franklinwh-direct-connect-bridge/blob/main/INSTALL.md).
- **Analytics → Explain:** the dialog's headings (Trends, What it shows, For your objectives, For a fuller answer) stand out, its sections are divided and spaced, and each suggestion sits in its own box, on a phone and on the desktop.
- **Analytics → Explain:** "For a fuller answer" has an **Open Billing & Utilities (Plans & meters)** button for the missing tariff, like the suggestions' Open buttons.
- **Analytics:** the **History** card shows the period in the chart (such as 9–15 Nov 2026), with how long the shown source has data for on its second line, on Local and Cloud and for every period.
- **Analytics:** for **Lifetime**, Line and Area are greyed in the Chart menu with the reason, and the chart shows bars: Lifetime is one total a year.

### Fixed
- **Import tariff:** after **Import** the dialog now says what was added and to which meter, with a **Done** button, instead of one small line under the preview and only the corner × to leave. **Check** can no longer be pressed again to import the same file a second time, which would have ended the plan just added (#654). See [Billing & Utilities](../guide/energy-and-costs.md).
- **Analytics → Explain:** a suggestion's effect says "less from the grid" or "more from the grid" (and "saved" or "more on the bill"), so **Backup ready**'s raise-the-reserve effect no longer reads "≈ −102 kWh a month from the grid — At most this much more from the grid".
- **Analytics:** the SoC % axis shows only when a SoC line is drawn; Week, Month, Year and Lifetime on Cloud (which has no SoC for them) and Local with SoC switched off no longer show an empty 0–100 axis.
- **Changelog:** the release index has its own **Releases** heading, so Home Assistant's update dialog no longer shows it under [Unreleased], and its link to the full notes is a full web address, which works from that dialog.
