{# First-run setup — a modal, opened once when nothing is configured. Shape borrowed from the Modbus Bridge's wizard so the two feel like one product: a centred dialog with a linear `step` flow, and INTENT first. Three doors, because the three reasons someone opens this app for the first time are genuinely different — connect real hardware, look around with a mock, or configure it by hand. It waits for the legal disclaimer: both are modals, and opening setup over an unaccepted disclaimer buries the one thing the user is obliged to read. Its z-index sits BELOW the disclaimer's deliberately, so if the ordering ever slips the mistake is visible rather than hidden. It can always be closed. Closing records `skipped`, which stops the prompt and disables nothing; Settings → Setup is the same content, reachable for ever. #}
